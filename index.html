01—About & Research Foundations
01.1 Identity & Focus

I am Sean Scott Sannino — a student at Maastricht University focusing on human-computer interaction, distributed systems, and ambient interfaces.

01.2 Philosophy of Form & Space

I am drawn to how software feels in the hand: building personal computing tools that remain quiet, direct, and unpretentious. I explore how digital interfaces can weave naturally into physical architectures and everyday spatial environments without demanding constant cognitive surrender.

01.3 Systems & Security Depth

Currently deepening my understanding of lower-level systems architecture, local-first protocols, and computer security (penetration testing fundamentals and defensive engineering).
